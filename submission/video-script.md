# Demo video script (≤ 2 min, optional)

Record inside Anna (real voices). Screen at 1280×800, plus a phone clip if possible.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Anna dashboard → open PodcastIt | "I save articles I never read. PodcastIt turns them into a podcast I actually finish." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Paste a real article link, pick 8 min, Casual, English; show the voices picker | "Paste a link, a PDF or text. Pick the length, the tone, the language and the two voices." |
| 0:25–0:45 | Press Make my episode; progress: reading → planning → writing → recording 12/60 | "It reads the source, plans the chapters and writes a real conversation. You see every step." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Press play while recording continues; transcript highlight follows; tap a later line | "You can start listening before it's done. The transcript follows along; tap any line to jump." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Speed 1.5×, jump to chapter 3 | "Speed control and chapters, like a real podcast app." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Go deeper on a chapter → 3 min → new episode appears | "Want more on one part? Go deeper makes a follow-up episode on just that chapter." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Library, then switch language to Arabic and show the RTL transcript | "Everything is saved to your library. It works in English, French, Spanish and Arabic." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Logo + "PodcastIt on the Anna App Store" | "PodcastIt — on the Anna App Store." |
